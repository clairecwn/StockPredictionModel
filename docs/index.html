Settle
This is a research project. Its signals are probabilities learned from 2001 to 2025 and can be wrong, so nothing here is advice to buy or sell.
Your plan

How Settle works

When a company is in the news, its stock usually jumps or drops in the first day or two and then settles, much as ripples spread and fade after a stone hits water. Settle finds the latest shock for each stock on the list and compares it with about 317,000 shocks from 2001 to 2025, to estimate how rough the next four weeks are likely to be and how much to put in.

What each part means

  • Buy or wait compares buying at today's close with waiting a few days. It rests on the expected move over the next five trading days, which is a small edge rather than a forecast you can count on.
  • Further dip is how much lower the stock may still go before its lowest close in the four weeks after the news.
  • When the low may come gives the chances that this lowest close has already happened, or will come in the next few trading days.
  • Bad case is a level the stock should fall below only about 1 time in 10 while you hold it for four weeks.
  • Safest, Balanced and Bold are three position sizes. Each one caps the chance of being down more than 2%, 4% or 8% of your budget.
  • Higher chance of a sharp further drop appears when a stock sits in the riskiest group of comparable past shocks.
  • The ripples behind each stock are drawn from its own numbers: a bigger shock makes more rings, and wider expected swings spread them further.

Earnings previews

When a quiet stock reports earnings within three weeks, Settle estimates how big the reaction is likely to be, learned from 147,000 past reports. It does not say which way the stock will move, because nothing in the data shows that this can be predicted.

Combining stocks

You can add up to three stocks to a plan. Their sizes shrink together, because stocks tend to fall at the same time in a sell-off.

How accurate it is

Each forecast was tested on years the model never saw during training. A rank correlation of 0 means no skill and 1 means a perfect ordering.
