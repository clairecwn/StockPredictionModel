Your plan

How Settle works

When a company is in the news, its stock usually jumps or drops in the first day or two and then settles. Settle looks for each stock's latest news shock and compares it with about 317,000 past shocks from 2001 to 2025, to judge what the next four weeks are likely to bring.

The call

Buy means buying today has tended to beat waiting a few days, and Wait means the opposite. The edge is small, so the call is best used to time an entry you were already planning, not as a reason to buy. A stock with no news in the last three weeks has no call, but its chart and risk are still shown.

The risk

The expected low is how far the price is likely to dip in the coming weeks. The bad case is a drop that only about 1 stock in 10 goes beyond. Both are tested on years the models never saw, and the bad case has been passed about as often as promised.

How much to put in

Pick a budget and a risk level, and Settle sizes the position so that a bad month stays within the loss you chose. You can add up to three stocks to a plan, and their sizes shrink together because stocks tend to fall at the same time in a sell-off.
