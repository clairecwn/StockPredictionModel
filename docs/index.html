How Settle works

When a company is in the news, its stock usually moves on the first day or two and then settles. Settle looks at each stock on the watchlist, finds its most recent news event, and compares it with about 317,000 similar events from 2001 to 2025.

What each part means

  • Buy or Wait compares buying today with waiting a few days. It is based on the expected move over the next five trading days.
  • Expected further dip is how much lower the stock may still go before its 20-day low.
  • Bad case is a level the stock should fall below only about 1 time in 10 while you hold it for 20 trading days.
  • Safest, Balanced and Bold are three position sizes. Each one limits the chance of losing more than 2%, 4% or 8% of your budget.
  • High drop risk means the stock is in the riskiest fifth of comparable past events.

Earnings previews

When a quiet stock has an earnings report within three weeks, Settle shows how big the reaction is likely to be, learned from 147,000 past reports. It cannot say which way the stock will move.

Combining stocks

Add up to three stocks to a plan. Their sizes shrink together, because stocks tend to fall at the same time in a sell-off.
